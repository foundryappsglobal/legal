About Glitchfall

Glitchfall is a tile puzzle where gravity changes direction, with no luck, no lives and no ads. Easy and the daily level are free, and Medium and Pro are a single payment. It plays offline: only the purchase and the optional Game Center leaderboard use the internet. There are no accounts.

Purchases

The app offers an optional purchase, paid once. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we cannot issue one on your behalf. To ask for a refund, open reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you already paid on another device, use Restore Purchases — it is in the app's Settings and on the purchase screen.

Your data

What you create in the app stays on your device, and there are no accounts. The privacy policy explains what does leave it: the purchase check and, if you use it, the Game Center leaderboard.

The app's Settings screen shows an anonymous identifier. To erase everything stored on the device, uninstall the app. If you want us to delete a purchase record, send that identifier: without it we have no way to find which record is yours.
