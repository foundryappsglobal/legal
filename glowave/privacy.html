Glowave Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026 · Español

At a glance. Glowave has no accounts and no servers of ours. What you create in the app stays on your device. We do not advertise to you, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Nothing leaves your device. The app makes no network requests of its own: there is no analytics SDK, no purchase SDK and no server of ours to talk to. We receive no data about you of any kind, and there is therefore no record of you for us to look up, export or delete.

We are a small independent developer. We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer, so privacy questions go to the address above and are answered by us directly.

2. What we never collect

Glowave does not ask for and does not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, precise or coarse location, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers. There is no sign-up and no login.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs and no purchase SDK. It does not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites, which is why Glowave will never show you Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

3. Data that stays on your device

The content you create in the app and your settings are written to the app's private storage on your device. We have no access to it and no copy of it.

If you have iCloud Backup enabled, iOS may include that storage in your device backup. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy and we cannot read it.

4. Your purchase

Glowave is a paid app with a single up-front price. There are no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Apple processes the purchase in the App Store, before the app ever runs on your device. We never see or receive your card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials, and the app itself plays no part in the transaction: it contains no purchase SDK and never asks Apple or anyone else about what you bought. Apple acts as an independent controller for that transaction under its own privacy policy.

Apple gives us aggregated sales reports — units sold per country per day, with no information about who bought. We cannot identify a buyer from them, and we do not try to.

5. Legal bases for processing (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We do not process any personal data about you, so there is no legal basis for us to state. That is not a way of saying the processing is minor: the app performs no network request of its own, so no personal data ever reaches us.

We do not rely on consent for anything, so there is no consent for you to withdraw. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

Apple's processing of your purchase, and of any diagnostics you choose to share with it, is Apple's own and is described in its privacy policy.

6. International transfers

We are established in Córdoba, Argentina. Because no personal data reaches us, no transfer of your personal data to us — international or otherwise — takes place.

7. How long data is kept

On-device data lasts until you delete it or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to retain or to expire.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive it in a portable format, and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

Stated honestly: we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or erase. If you write to us we will tell you exactly that. We are not withholding anything behind an identifier — there is no identifier and no record.

To delete all on-device data, use Erase local data in the app's Settings, or uninstall the app; iOS erases its private storage.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue one ourselves.

We respond to requests within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Notice for California residents

Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, in the past 12 months we have collected no categories of personal information from users of Glowave. We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising — so there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link to provide, because there is nothing to opt out of.

You have the right to know, delete, and correct your personal information, and to be free from retaliation for exercising those rights. Requests go to foundry.apps.global@gmail.com; our answer will be that we hold nothing. Authorised agents may submit requests with written proof of authorisation.

Residents of other US states with comparable laws — including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon — have substantially the same rights and may use the same contact address.

10. Children

Glowave is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age of consent. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Crash diagnostics

The app includes no third-party crash reporting. If you have enabled "Share iPhone Analytics" or "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may send us aggregated, anonymous crash reports. That sharing is controlled by you in iOS, not by this app, and you can turn it off there at any time.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above. If a change materially affects how your data is handled — for example if we ever add analytics — we will say so inside the app before it takes effect, and update our Apple privacy disclosures to match.
