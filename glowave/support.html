About Glowave: Light Text

Glowave turns the screen into a brush: you type a word, sweep the phone through the air, and persistence of vision leaves it written where your arm passed. With Pro you can also stand the phone up and let the text scroll across the screen on its own. The text stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.

Subscriptions

The app offers an optional subscription. Cancelling, changing plan and requesting a refund are handled by Apple, not by us — we cannot do any of the three on your behalf. Open your Apple Account subscriptions to cancel or switch plans, and reportaproblem.apple.com to ask for a refund.

If you already paid on another device, use Restore Purchases — it is in the app's Settings and on the subscription screen.

Your data

What you create in the app stays on your device, and there are no accounts. The privacy policy explains the one thing that does leave it.

The app's Settings screen shows an anonymous identifier and a button to erase everything stored on the device. If you want us to delete a subscription record, send that identifier: without it we have no way to find which record is yours.
